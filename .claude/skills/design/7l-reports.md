### 7k. Reports Screen
Page title: "Rapports" H1

Tab bar: CA | TVA | Charges | Comparatif
Tab CA (Chiffre d'affaires):
- Period selector: month/quarter/year
- KPI row: CA total, CA moyen/mois, Croissance %
- Chart: bar chart monthly revenue 12 months
- Table below: month | CA HT | TVA | TTC
- Export button top right

Tab TVA:
- KPI row: TVA collectée, TVA déductible, TVA nette
- Chart: stacked bar collectée vs déductible
- Table: month | collectée | déductible | nette
- Negative nette highlighted blue (crédit)

Tab Charges:
- KPI row: Total charges, Moyenne/mois
- Chart: line chart 12 months
- Breakdown: pie chart by category
- Table: catégorie | montant | % du total

Tab Comparatif:
- 2 period selectors side by side
- KPI cards showing delta between periods
- Chart: overlay line chart both periods
- Table: metric | period 1 | period 2 | variation %
- Green positive, red negative
