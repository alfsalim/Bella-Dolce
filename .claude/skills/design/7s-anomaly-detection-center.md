### 7s. Anomaly Detection Center
Access: sidebar nav icon:ShieldAlert
Page title: "Anomalies & Alertes" H1
Top right: "Marquer tout lu" ghost

Summary row: 4 mini cards
- Critiques: count red
- Avertissements: count amber
- Suggestions: count blue
- Résolues ce mois: count green

Anomaly list:
- Card per anomaly
- Left: severity icon (red/amber/blue)
- Title: "TVA déductible incohérente"
- Description: "Montant +45% vs moyenne"
- Source: linked invoice/transaction
- Date détectée: timestamp
- Status: Nouveau | En cours | Résolu | Ignoré
- Actions: Voir détails | Résoudre | Ignorer

Detail view (expand or page):
- Full explanation from AI
- Visual: chart or diff showing anomaly
- Affected documents: linked list
- AI recommendation: card with action
- Resolution: dropdown + comment + confirm

Filter bar:
- Severity: all/critique/warning/suggestion
- Status: all/nouveau/en cours/résolu
- Period: date range
- Source: factures/banque/G50
